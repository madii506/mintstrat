How MintStrat works.

MintStrat is a strategy coin for Pokémon cards. Its creator fees buy graded Pokémon cards on chain, every card is relisted above what the vault paid, and every sale buys back and burns $MINTSTRAT. This page writes the rules down so anyone can hold the strategy to them.

The loop

  1. Fees stack. Every $MINTSTRAT trade on pump.fun pays a creator fee. 100% of it goes into the strategy vault, one public Solana wallet.
  2. Cop a grail. When the vault holds enough SOL, it buys a graded Pokémon card that lives on Solana as a token backed by the real slab.
  3. Relist higher. The card is listed right away at 1.2× what the vault paid. It stays listed until someone buys it.
  4. Flip and burn. When a card sells, 100% of the SOL buys $MINTSTRAT on the open market and burns it.

The rules

  • Only graded Pokémon cards that trade on chain on Solana. No raw cards, no other games.
  • One vault. Every card the strategy owns sits in the same public wallet, published here at launch.
  • Listed at the markup. Cards are listed at 1.2× cost, so a sale always means more SOL came back than went out.
  • Burns are on chain. A burn is a real token burn, so it shows up in the token's supply, not just in a post.
  • Receipts for everything. Every buy, listing, sale and burn is a transaction from the vault wallet.

What you can check yourself

  • Supply burned: 1,000,000,000 minus the token's current supply, read from Solana.
  • Vault balance: the vault wallet's SOL, read from Solana.
  • Cards in the vault: the tokens the vault holds, read from Magic Eden's public wallet index.
  • Market cap, volume and price: from Dexscreener.

Before launch those numbers read —. They are never typed in by hand.

What it isn't

  • MintStrat is independent. It is not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK, The Pokémon Company or any grading company.
  • Card prices can fall and a listed card can take time to sell. A strategy doesn't make anything risk free.
  • Nothing here is financial advice. Memecoins are risky; only use what you can afford to lose.
