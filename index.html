Every $MINTSTRAT trade pays a creator fee. 100% of it flows into the strategy vault, one public wallet anyone can watch.

02

When the vault can afford one, it buys a graded Pokémon card on chain: a real slab, vaulted and tokenized, so it trades in seconds.

06 — questions

Before you cop.

What is MintStrat?
A strategy coin for Pokémon cards. Its creator fees buy graded cards on chain, each card is relisted above what the vault paid, and when it sells the SOL buys back and burns $MINTSTRAT.
Which cards does it buy?
Graded Pokémon cards that live on Solana as tokens backed by the real slab in a vault. That makes them buyable and sellable in seconds, with every trade on chain.
Where are the cards kept?
The tokens sit in one public vault wallet, linked on this page at launch. The physical slabs are held by the platform that tokenized them.
Why relist instead of holding?
A held card just sits there. A card listed above cost turns into a burn the moment someone buys it, so the binder keeps working for holders.
What does the burn do?
Burned tokens are gone for good. Every sale shrinks the supply, so each $MINTSTRAT left is a bigger share of what's next.
Can I check every move?
Yes. Every buy, listing and burn is a transaction from the vault wallet. The live section links the wallet, the token and the chart at launch.
Is MintStrat connected to Pokémon or Nintendo?
No. MintStrat is independent and not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK, The Pokémon Company or any grading company. It just buys the cards.
